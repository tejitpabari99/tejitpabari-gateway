## Overview
Automates the tedious process of downloading movies and shows from pahe.ink. The tool would streamline browsing content, fetching mega links, and downloading files locally or to cloud storage.

## Features
- Browse movies and shows on pahe.ink website
- Automatically fetch mega links by navigating through the required steps and pages
- Auto-download from mega with virus protection
- Download to local hard drive or cloud storage
- Upload to central storage location
- Manage download limits for free mega accounts (stops at daily GB limit, resumes next day)
- Optional upload to central store

## Future Ideas
- TV app for viewing downloaded content
- Web browser interface
- Integration with gateway website

## Notes
Currently requires manually clicking through multiple pages, waiting, and downloading each episode individually, which is very time-consuming.
